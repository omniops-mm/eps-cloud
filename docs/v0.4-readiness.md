## Release blockers

- Argo CD 3.5.2 maintained image: 4 fixable HIGH findings in PCRE2/SQLite. Chart 10.9.0 configuration is under `deploy/platform/pending/`, not the installable registry. Runtime compatibility and effective permissions remain unproven until a cleared image can run.
- Current official k3s 1.36.4+k3s1 image: 26 fixable findings. No VM was created to wait on a correction. Cloud deployment, its IAM/private-access evidence and production GitOps cannot be marked complete.

Prometheus Adapter 0.12.0 passed filesystem and verified signed-inventory checks and replaces the unselected KEDA route. Image review is time-specific; recheck exact digests before deployment. Docker attestation verification used its published key with `--skip-tlog`; transparency verification is not claimed. Finding counts are scanner occurrences, not proven exploit paths. No vulnerability exclusions or custom forks were added.

Local workload/data credentials remain in Kubernetes, not source files. Probe tunnels, temporary Jobs/namespaces and canaries are cleaned up. Production promotion stays disabled. Keep package/chart version metadata and release tags unchanged until the remaining acceptance gates pass.

The release-gate refresh on 13 September confirmed the current k3s scan still reports 26 findings. The Argo 3.5.2 DHI digest is unchanged; an upstream 3.5.2 scan reports 113 findings and the maintained DHI 3.4.8 alternative reports four. These alternatives do not clear the deployment gate. CI repository-write permissions and the bot promotion implementation have been removed; the operator verifies and commits production snapshots.
